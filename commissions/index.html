How requests work

Clear approval points prevent surprise changes and keep the queue manageable.

Before requesting

Basic terms

A commission is custom digital artwork, not an unlimited revision service.

  • Prices are in USD and cover personal use unless stated otherwise.
  • A 50% deposit reserves the slot; the balance is due before final delivery.
  • One focused sketch revision round and minor color corrections are included.
  • Large pose, outfit, or design changes after sketch approval require a new quote.
  • Turnaround is normally 3–8 weeks after work begins, depending on scope and queue.
  • Final files are delivered digitally. Source files are not included by default.
  • I retain authorship and portfolio rights unless privacy is agreed on beforehand.
Send a request
What should I include?

Include clear character references, the format you want, pose or mood ideas, intended use, and anything that must not be changed.

Do you draw mature work?

Yes, for verified adult clients and adult characters. I may decline themes I am not comfortable depicting. Mature examples remain labeled and separated from general work.

What will you not draw?

I do not accept hateful content, exploitative depictions of minors, non-consensual sexual content presented as erotic, or projects intended to impersonate another artist.

Can the commission stay private?

Ask before payment. Temporary privacy may be included; permanent non-posting or NDA-style restrictions may require an additional fee.

Are refunds available?

Before work begins, the unspent portion can be refunded. After work begins, refunds are based on the amount of completed work. Completed artwork is non-refundable.
